# ClinicOS - simple presentation and demo script

Use the four diagrams in order. The main demo takes about 10 minutes; add the optional operations tour for a fuller presentation.

## Before presenting

- Use only fictional records prefixed DEMO. Keep real patient information off screen.
- Have the relevant individual demo accounts ready; do not put passwords in slides or share one account between staff.
- Choose **DEMO · Ava** for a populated signed clinical record, or **DEMO · Rona** / **DEMO · Shilan** for an existing draft. Use the same patient throughout your core demonstration.
- Existing seeded appointments are anchored to **25 October 2026**, with later examples on 26 and 27 October. Choose those dates to show existing examples; they will not necessarily appear under today's schedule.
- For a live new appointment, choose an available time and clinician. Record actual booking conflicts as conflicts rather than forcing the reservation.
- Have one fictional PDF on the computer for the upload example. A scan must first be saved as PDF. The file limit is 10 MB; the existing media consent and scanning checks apply.
- Use print preview and cancel it when demonstrating printing. Use fictional amounts and saved local demo records for financial examples.

## Diagram 1 - the patient journey

Say: "ClinicOS follows the patient from reception to treatment, payment and follow-up. Each staff member signs in with their own account."

Point to the six steps. Explain that this is a typical workflow, not an enforced order for every action. Photos, reports and consent can be added when needed during care.

## Diagram 2 - the patient profile

Say: "The patient profile is where we find the patient's visits, history, documents and photographs. We confirm the name and patient number before working."

Point out the seven sections. Explain the distinction:

- **Lab reports & documents:** give an uploaded PDF a useful title such as "DEMO blood test". Open the saved report, then choose **Open PDF**.
- **Clinical media:** browse thumbnail photographs in a four-column desktop gallery, up to 16 files per page. Open a tile, use Previous/Next or arrow keys, then press Escape to close.
- **Consent & care documents:** review consent and signed referrals, care letters or aftercare documents.

## Diagram 3 - staff responsibilities

Say: "Everyone sees the tools their job requires. Business administration does not automatically give access to clinical records or photographs."

Show the five role columns. Assigned-care settings may further restrict clinical access. The diagram is a simple guide to typical responsibilities; actual access follows the account's permissions.

## Diagram 4 - the live demo

Use existing completed examples when you want a read-only demo. Signing a draft or posting a payment creates a lasting event, even in the local demo.

## Optional operations tour - about 5 minutes

1. **Administrator / Inventory:** show product catalog, suppliers, a lot with expiry, stock alerts and a saved stock-use record. Explain receive stock -> use stock -> review alerts.
2. **Accountant / Finance and Reports:** show expenses/payables, cash movements and till close. Run a report in the correct date range; keep IQD and USD totals separate. Mention deposits, packages and refunds as supported workflows.
3. **Accountant / Accounting:** point out bank/card reconciliation, approved account mappings, batch CSV exports and period close. Importing a statement does not itself create a payment.
4. **Owner / Treatments and Team & access:** show treatment price approvals, individual accounts and permitted access controls. Point out Settings and the audit trail without changing permissions during the presentation.
5. Switch English/Sorani, then open **Help & user guide**. Sign out when finished.

Closing sentence: "ClinicOS keeps patient care, documents and clinic operations connected, with each staff member working through their own account."

## Short answers for common questions

- **Can it scan paper directly?** Save a PDF using the computer's scanner software, then upload it to the patient profile.
- **Does it send appointment messages?** The reminder workflow supports staff call lists and manual call logs; it does not place calls or send SMS/WhatsApp.
- **Can signed records be edited?** Corrections use addenda or corrected documents, preserving the original.
- **Can issued invoices or payments be erased?** Use the authorized credit/refund/correction workflow.
- **Can IQD and USD be added together?** They stay separate unless an explicit recorded conversion is used.
